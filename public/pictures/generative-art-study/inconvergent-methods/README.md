# Generative method shortlist

The strongest original implementations of techniques documented in [On Generative Algorithms](https://inconvergent.net/generative/), rendered in the OpenTaint palette. Asemic Spline Script, Linetrace Spline, Offset Spline Creatures, and Seeded Fracture Field are intentionally excluded from this review.

1. **Hyphae growth study** — Non-overlapping connected circles with shrinking radii, wobble, and perpendicular side branches.  
   [PNG](./hyphae-growth.png) · [SVG](./hyphae-growth.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/hyphae/; https://github.com/inconvergent/hyphae)

2. **Tip-growth tree study** — A tip-only branch queue whose widths shrink while angular wobble grows with age.  
   [PNG](./tip-growth-trees.png) · [SVG](./tip-growth-trees.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/trees/; https://github.com/inconvergent/tree)

3. **Differential Line** — Curvature-weighted edge splitting with linked attraction and non-linked repulsion.  
   [PNG](./differential-line.png) · [SVG](./differential-line.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/differential-line/)

4. **Differential Mesh** — Outward triangle growth with free-space tests, mesh relaxation, and long-edge splitting.  
   [PNG](./differential-mesh.png) · [SVG](./differential-mesh.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/differential-mesh/)

5. **Differential Lattice** — Offset node spawning with mutual-nearest links and coupled spring/rejection forces.  
   [PNG](./differential-lattice.png) · [SVG](./differential-lattice.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/differential-lattice/)

6. **Accumulating spline drift** — Sand Spline study: equal-sampled closed splines accumulate outward-weighted radial drift into a layered trace.  
   [PNG](./accumulating-spline-drift.png) · [SVG](./accumulating-spline-drift.svg) · [Method](https://inconvergent.net/generative/sand-spline/)
