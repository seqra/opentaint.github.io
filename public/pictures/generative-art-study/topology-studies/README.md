# Topology studies

9 deterministic mesh and lattice relatives in the OpenTaint palette. Connected Tree Forest, Constrained Void Field, Relaxed Ribbon Archipelago, and Triangulated Flow Network are intentionally excluded from this review.

1. **Triangulated Terrain Path** — A continuous oblique terrain mesh with visible peaks, valleys, and one edge-following path.  
   [PNG](./triangulated-terrain-path.png) · [SVG](./triangulated-terrain-path.svg)

2. **Adaptive proximity lattice** — Relative-neighborhood links adapt to local spacing and resolve into one coherent route.  
   [PNG](./adaptive-proximity-lattice.png) · [SVG](./adaptive-proximity-lattice.svg)

3. **Force-relaxed branching graph** — A depth hierarchy settles under spring attraction and bounded repulsion, with a sparse connected red branch.  
   [PNG](./force-relaxed-branching.png) · [SVG](./force-relaxed-branching.svg)

4. **Centered force-relaxed tree** — A single force-relaxed rooted tree is centered at broad scale, with one non-trivial red route to an interior canopy leaf.  
   [PNG](./two-tree-leaf-bridge.png) · [SVG](./two-tree-leaf-bridge.svg)

5. **Opposed tree leaf bridge** — Opposed floor- and ceiling-rooted trees meet through selected facing leaves and one continuous red route.  
   [PNG](./opposed-tree-leaf-bridge.png) · [SVG](./opposed-tree-leaf-bridge.svg)

6. **Deformed stress lattice** — A smooth deformation field turns a structural grid into a readable map of local strain.  
   [PNG](./deformed-stress-lattice.png) · [SVG](./deformed-stress-lattice.svg)

7. **Diagonal stress lattice** — A sheared diagonal structural field exposes strain along intersecting lattice directions.  
   [PNG](./diagonal-stress-lattice.png) · [SVG](./diagonal-stress-lattice.svg)

8. **Voronoi–Delaunay dual** — Clipped cells and their generating graph share one jittered field, with a single red chain crossing the dual reading.  
   [PNG](./voronoi-delaunay-dual.png) · [SVG](./voronoi-delaunay-dual.svg)

9. **Clustered polygonal topology** — Weighted local meshes become connected islands through deliberately chosen bridges and one restrained red connection.  
   [PNG](./clustered-polygonal-topology.png) · [SVG](./clustered-polygonal-topology.svg)
